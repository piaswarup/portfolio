VEX Robotics Team

Founding and leading my high school's VEX Robotics team.

Overview

From September 2023 to June 2025, I founded and led my high school's VEX Robotics team, which designs, builds, and programs robots for the VEX Robotics Competition. I started it because I wanted to apply the math and physics I was learning in class to real-world problems. At a mostly business-focused high school, growing the team was a challenge, but it grew to five committed members.

My role

As Founder & President, I led robot design and assembly, including the chassis and mechanical systems. Off the field, I built the team into something that could last: I led fundraising, budgeting, technical documentation, and the team's infrastructure.

Design & build

We designed and built our intake, pneumatic grabbing, catapult, conveyor belt, and launching mechanisms from scratch, and programmed the autonomous and driver controls in Python.

The mechanism I'm proudest of picked up donut-shaped game pieces and placed them on a stake. A conveyor belt with a rubber hook-like part caught the inside of each donut and pulled it up, then an alignment jig at the top released it straight onto the stake.

Results

  • Placed in the top 10 at the 2024 and 2025 VEX Robotics Benelux Championships
  • Founded and grew a sustainable team through fundraising, budgeting, and technical documentation

What I learned

The team was completely student-led. Our math teacher was our main point of contact at the school but didn't help run the club, and finding a mentor willing to help us unpaid was extremely difficult. I learned how hard it is to motivate people to commit time outside busy school and sports schedules, to find meeting times that work for everyone, and to coordinate with the school so we could use a space after class hours.
