Karate

Black belt and multiple international medalist.

I'm a black belt and multiple international medalist, and I was a member of the Belgian national team for kumite (free sparring) for several years.

I've trained in karate since I was 6, for more than 11 years. My karate journey reflects years of hard work and consistency: training five or more times a week for over 10 years, with frequent competitions. Karate is how I take my mind off work and school. It keeps me physically fit and trains my mental strength: endurance, agility, and performing in front of hundreds of people.
